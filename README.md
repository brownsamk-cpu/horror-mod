# Horror Mod

A spooky-fun (Halloween, not scary) Fabric mod for Minecraft 26.3, built one step at a time.

## Step 1: the Ghost (first called the Spooky Spirit)
- `horrormod:spooky_spirit`: a small, **clear, see-through** ghost.
- It floats slowly, drifts around, bobs up and down, and looks at you.
- Now and then it says a soft "wooo" (subtitle: "Ghost goes wooo").
- It is passive: it never attacks or hurts anyone.
- It has a spawn egg and spawns rarely, only in the dark (at night or in caves).

The texture, spawn egg, icon, and sound are defined as data in `tools/spooky_spirit.py`.

Build: `./gradlew build` (runs the game tests). Fabric Loader 0.19.5, Fabric API 0.163.0+26.3, Java 25.

## Step 2 (0.2.0): Super Phantom

`horrormod:super_phantom` - "like a phantom, but worse". A big, dark purple/black phantom with glowing cyan eyes that flies
and swoops slowly around at night. (0.2.0 was peaceful; see 0.3.0 below.) Its trick: when it swoops within about 2 blocks of a
survival/adventure player, it snatches **one** item from a random hotbar/main-inventory slot (never your armor or offhand),
with a whoosh, purple sparkles, smoke, and the subtitle "Super Phantom snatches an item". Then it has to wait about 30 seconds.
It keeps what it stole (it won't despawn while holding your things) and drops **everything** it took when it is defeated.

- Rare: only at night under the open sky. Spawn egg: "Super Phantom Spawn Egg" (next to the Phantom egg in Creative).
- Texture, glowing eyes, spawn egg, and the whoosh sound are all data in `tools/super_phantom.py`.
- Previews: `previews/super_phantom_*.png`.

### 0.3.0: the Super Phantom is now HOSTILE
Same look, size, egg, and sound. Now it is a monster: at night it hunts survival/adventure players, swooping in to bite for
about 2 hp per hit on Normal (less on Easy, more on Hard, like vanilla), and it does not spawn on Peaceful. It still snatches
one item (30 s cooldown) and drops everything it stole when defeated (or if the world is switched to Peaceful).

## 0.4.0 — Mysterious Witch

A new mob, the **Mysterious Witch** (`horrormod:mysterious_witch`): purple-and-teal starry robes, a tall crooked starry hat, and glowing green eyes. She's **neutral**. She wanders around and ignores you until you hit her, then throws her own splash potions at you for about 30 seconds and calms down after that. She spawns rarely at night in dark forests, pale gardens and swamps, comes with a spawn egg, cackles (subtitle "Mysterious Witch cackles"), and sometimes (40%) drops one of her potions.

Four new potions, each drinkable or splash, are in the Creative tabs:

| Potion | Effect |
|---|---|
| Bouncy Potion | You bounce back up every time you land (and take no fall damage). |
| Glow Potion | You glow and leave a trail of sparkles. |
| Bat Potion | Harmless little bats swirl around you and the screen gets a spooky purple tint. |
| Slime Feet Potion | You slide around like you're on ice. |

The texture, potion colours and icons, egg and cackle are generated by `tools/mysterious_witch.py`.
Previews: `previews/mysterious_witch_closeup.png`, `mysterious_witch_throwing.png`, `mysterious_witch_potions_inventory.png`, `mysterious_witch_spawn_egg_big.png`.

## 0.9.0 — Stony Cave and the Stone Monster (June and Leon's design)

- **Stony Cave** (`horrormod:stony_cave`): right-click and a rocky cave appears in front of you. It's "50 percent
  scary": dim (only two torches by the mouth, a few glowing lichen patches), cobwebs and dripstone in the dark corners,
  and creepy cave sounds when the boss is near.
- **Stone Monster** (`horrormod:stone_monster`, spawn egg `horrormod:stone_monster_spawn_egg`): a 5-block-tall rocky mini
  boss with glowing red eyes. It winds up and throws rocks (2 hearts each) and slams the ground with both fists if you get
  close. A boss health bar shows while you're nearby. 150 health. It guards the Stone Door.
- **Stone Key** (`horrormod:stone_key`): the Stone Monster always drops it. Right-click the **Stone Door**
  (`horrormod:stone_door`) with it and the door rumbles open (you keep the key).
- Behind the door: a **snowy** room (snow, ice, spruce), a **jungle** room (jungle leaves, vines, moss) and a **desert**
  room (sand, sandstone, cactus), each with a little reward chest.
- Textures: `tools/stone_monster.py`. Layout: `StonyCave.java`.
